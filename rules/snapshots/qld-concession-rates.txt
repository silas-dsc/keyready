Transfer duty home concession rates
These home concession rates reduce the amount of transfer (stamp) duty you pay when you acquire vacant land or a residence that you’ll use as your home.

When you have land in Queensland transferred to you that you’ll use as your home, you may be eligible to claim a concession that reduces the amount of transfer (stamp) duty you have to pay on the dutiable value of the property.
Below are the rates for the home, first home, first home (new home) and first home vacant land concessions. (The transfer duty rates apply if you’re not eligible for a concession or exemption .)
There are no additional concessions or exemptions for seniors card or pensioner concession card holders.
Additional duty of 8% applies to acquisitions of residential land by foreign persons (including companies and trusts). Find out more about the additional foreign acquirer duty .
To calculate the first home concession, you first use the home concession rate and then subtract the first home concession amount.

Home concession
The home concession was not available from 1 August 2011 to 30 June 2012.

Calculation example
You have previously owned a home before, and you sign an agreement to buy a house in Queensland that will be your next home. The amount you agree to pay is $950,000. You will occupy the home immediately after settlement, so you claim a home concession for the transfer duty.
Because the value of the home is within the $540,000 to $1,000,000 range of the home concession rate, duty is assessed at $10,150 plus $4.50 for each $100, or part of $100, over $540,000.
This is how the duty is calculated:
$10,150 (for the first $540,000)
+ $18,450 ($4.50 for every $100 in $410,000, the balance above $540,000)
= $28,600 (the transfer duty using the home concession rate)

Calculation example—contract signed after 9 June 2024
You sign an agreement to buy a house in Queensland that will be your first home. The amount you agree to pay is $795,000. You will occupy the home immediately after settlement, so you claim a first home concession for the transfer duty.
Because the value of the home is within the $540,000 to $1,000,000 range of the home concession rate, duty is first assessed at $10,150 plus $4.50 for each $100, or part of $100, over $540,000. The first home concession amount is then subtracted from the result.
This is how the duty is calculated:
$10,150 (for the first $540,000)
+ $11,475 ($4.50 for every $100 in $255,000, the balance above $540,000)
= $21,625 (the transfer duty using the home concession rate)
– $1,735 (the first home concession amount for homes valued between $790,000 to $799,999.99)
= $19,890

First home (new home) concession
For the first home (new home) concession —for eligible transactions entered into on or after 1 May 2025 —a full transfer duty concession is available. There is no cap on the purchase price (value) of the new home for first home buyers. However, the concession only applies to residential land, and transfer duty will be imposed at the transfer duty rates on any additional land that doesn’t have a residence on it or isn’t used for residential purposes.
First home vacant land concession
For the first home vacant land concession —for eligible transactions entered into on or after 1 May 2025 —a full transfer duty concession is available for first home buyers. There is no cap on the value of the vacant land; however, the concession will only apply to residential vacant land. Transfer duty will be imposed at the transfer duty rates on any additional land that won’t have a residence constructed on it or used for residential purposes.
